# flight-ui: merged visual QA work list (wave 2)

**Verdict.** Photo lens 5.5 / 10, artifact lens 6.5 / 10 (34 screenshots each). The HUD has a sound base (consistent panels, clean type, complete data, crisp DOM text at 4K), but it is not yet at the level of a shipped flight game, because its primary instruments give wrong or unreadable information in ordinary states: the G gauge shows red at rest, the navball is a clutter of numerals whenever the nose points straight up or down, the whole bottom cluster jumps off the centre line while a maneuver node exists, panels let the scene print through their text, zero vertical speed looks like a missing glyph, and the altimeter says "sea level" on the Moon. The cockpit MFD pages are label / value tables with underbuilt instruments. This list merges the 28 findings of the two lenses (14 each) into 19 items: 10 major, 9 minor. I opened the cited screenshots for every item (full frames, enlarged crops, and pixel measurements where a number was claimed). Every defect is visible and no item was dropped. Two claims are extrapolations that no screenshot can show; they are kept as test-coverage gaps and marked as such (the throttle fill in item 1, label contrast over a white backdrop in item 4).

**How to read this list.** Items are ordered by how much a player would notice. Where the two lenses rated one defect differently, the item carries the higher rating and names both. "Checked" says what I saw when I opened the screenshots. The last section maps every source finding to its item.

### 1. The G LOAD tube ends in red at every reading

- **Severity:** major (photo: major, artifact: major)
- **Screenshots:** 1.00 g: `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\pad-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\pad-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\stage-lockout.png`. 0.17 g: `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\lunarSurface-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\lunarSurface-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\terrain-pending-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\terrain-pending-720p.png`. 4.50 g: `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\reentry-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\reentry-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\failed-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\failed-720p.png`. Throttle: `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\ascent-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\ascent-720p.png`.
- **Defect:** The green-amber-red ramp is squeezed into whatever height is filled, so the top of the fill is always red. On the pad at 1.00 g the 10 %-high fill runs from green to a red cap; on the Moon at 0.17 g it is a 2 % orange sliver; at a nominal 4.50 g entry the same complete ramp ends at 45 % of the tube. The colour carries no information and signals "at the limit" at rest. Neither tube has numerals or a limit mark (full scale is a silent 10 g, ten unlabelled ticks), and the fill ends in a hard flat cut inside a round-ended tube. No screenshot shows the throttle fill at all: the powered-ascent scene reads 0 % throttle and 0.00 g at Mach 1.17 (FakeSim data).
- **Checked:** Confirmed. pad-4k crop: green base, flat red top at the first tick. reentry-4k crop: the full ramp ends just below the fifth tick. lunarSurface-4k crop at 6x: the sliver contains the whole green-to-red ramp. ascent-720p: 0 %, 0.00 g, M 1.17, both tubes empty. The throttle fill cannot be judged from this set (coverage gap, not a visible defect).
- **Real reference:** Real g-meters and engine tapes (Shuttle MEDS tapes, Orion and Crew Dragon displays, KSP's g gauge) have colour bands fixed to the scale: green up to a limit, amber and red zones at fixed values, a pointer or fill moving against them, and printed numerals. A 1 g reading sits entirely in green.
- **Likely cause:** `src/ui/hud/hudStyles.ts:261-262`: `.gauge .tube > i` is a full-height element and `.gauge.g .tube > i` gives it `linear-gradient(to top, #5fe39a, #ffbb4d 55%, #ff6262)`. `FillSlot` (`src/ui/hud/dom.ts`, created at `src/ui/hud/hud.ts:387-390`) scales that element with `transform: scaleY(f)` (`hud.ts:758`, `gFill.set(r.gLoad / 10)`), so the gradient scales with the fill. `hudStyles.ts:263` draws the ticks without labels. The throttle fill is built the same way (its cyan gradient compresses too, harmlessly).
- **Fix direction:** Keep the gradient static at full tube height and only reveal it, with compositor-only transforms: a fixed gradient layer under a dark cover that is anchored at the top and scaled by (1 - f), or an overflow-hidden wrapper translated by (1 - f) x 100 % with the gradient child counter-translated. Put the zone stops at fixed g values from one shared set of thresholds. Today `src/ui/mfd/mfdPainter.ts:110` tones the FLT row at 4 / 6 g while `:188` and `:517` tone the ENT row and bar at 5 / 8 g; pick one set (the sim's real limits) and use it in the HUD and on both MFD pages. Add numerals 0 / 5 / 10 g and 0 / 50 / 100 % beside the tubes and a limit tick, and round the top of the fill. Add a lab decoration with throttle > 0 and a matching g load, and re-shoot the ascent scene.

- **Defect:** With the nose at the zenith or nadir (on the pad, in vertical ascent, landed on the Moon, under parachutes: the first and last views of every mission) the ball shows six spokes of numerals, the upper three upside down. The six "80" labels, each pre-stretched about 5.8 times, are packed into a ring that sits under the reticle, so half-hidden "8" and "80" fragments poke out around the orange wings. The zenith / nadir mark is completely covered by the reticle hub and the prograde ring, so the one thing this attitude should show (where straight up is) cannot be seen. Two tests sample sky colour around the reticle at the zenith and will need to follow the new pole mark: `tests/unit/flight-ui/navball.test.ts:37-41` and `tests/browser/flight-ui/controls.spec.ts:467`. ### 3. A maneuver node pushes the navball and the whole bottom cluster off the centre line

- **Severity:** major (photo: major, artifact: major)
- **Screenshots:** With a node: `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\lowOrbit-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\lowOrbit-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\map-lowOrbit-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\map-lowOrbit-4k.png`. Without a node it is centred. Creating or deleting a node therefore makes the primary flight instrument jump sideways by about 4 % of the frame height in one frame and leaves it misaligned under the altitude panel and the vessel. In the map view the MANEUVER box hangs off the lower right of the ball with empty space on the left. Even without a node the ball sits 4 px right of centre at 4K.
- **Checked:** Confirmed by measuring the ball between its bezel edges: map-lowOrbit-720p centre x = 612.5 to 613.0, map-lowOrbit-4k 1838.5; lowOrbit-720p ball spans about x = 510 to 716; prograde-hold and extra-rig are centred (ball about 538 to 744). - **Real reference:** KSP, KSP 2 and every flight HUD keep the attitude ball locked to the screen centre line; burn information attaches beside the ball (KSP's burn read-out on the navball's right) without moving it.
- **Likely cause:** `src/ui/hud/hudStyles.ts:230`: `.bottom` is a flex row centred as a whole with `left: 50%; translateX(-50%)`. `src/ui/hud/hud.ts:405-408`: `rightStack` holds the 25u-wide node panel (`hudStyles.ts:269`) above the roughly 17u gauges panel, so the row's right wing widens by about 8u when the node panel is shown and the row's centre moves about 4u. Add a browser assertion that the ball's bounding-box centre equals the viewport centre within 1 px with and without `node=1`, in the external and map layouts.

### 4. Panels are see-through: the scene prints through the text and labels miss 4.5 : 1

- **Severity:** major (photo: major, artifact: major)
- **Screenshots:** `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\pad-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\pad-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\stage-lockout.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\ascent-720p.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\ascent-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\chutes-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\reentry-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\translunar-4k.png`, `C:\dev\fable-space-game\out\screens\flight-ui\cockpit-lowOrbit-4k.png`
- **Defect:** Panels are 66 % opaque with no backdrop treatment, so the scene sets their brightness. Over the lab's pastel sky (about (158, 187, 224)) a panel becomes about (62, 74, 89) and the secondary text colour `#93a2b8` (row labels, compact-tile delta-v, TWR / burn line, UT clock, vertical-speed line) measures 3.4 to 4.1 : 1 against it, below the 4.5 : 1 expected of 10 to 11 px capitals. The scene is recognisable through the plates: the horizon line runs across the LAUNCH panel's last row, the white rocket is a bright vertical band directly behind "SEA LEVEL" and "60.0 m", and stars sit between rows over space. Over black space the panels all but vanish (fill (5, 8, 12) on black, 17 %-alpha hairline), so blocks of numbers float with no container. Primary white values are fine (7.9 : 1).
- **Checked:** Confirmed. pad-4k crops: the horizon crosses the "LAUNCH AZIMUTH" row and the rocket shows as a light band behind the altitude reading. My own samples: label (147, 162, 184) on panel (60, 69, 81) = 3.75 : 1 ("RELATIVE INCLINATION", pad-4k), 3.81 (compact delta-v), 4.0 ("MISSION TIME"); pad-720p 3.47 and 3.64. translunar-4k and cockpit-lowOrbit-4k crops: stars between the rows, panel edge a faint hairline. - **Real reference:** Shipped flight interfaces over bright scenes (Microsoft Flight Simulator, KSP 2, Elite Dangerous, Star Citizen MFDs) use near-opaque plates (85 to 95 %) or frosted glass (blurred and darkened backdrop), hold secondary text at 4.5 : 1 or better in the worst-case scene, keep a visible panel edge on bright and dark backgrounds alike, and let nothing behind a plate be recognisable through it.

Lighten `--label` to about `#aebccf` and stop using `--dim` for anything informative, so labels hold 4.5 : 1 over a white backdrop. Give panels a slightly lighter fill floor and a 1 px inner highlight so the edge reads over black. Add a lab backdrop mode with a near-white (250) cloud deck or lit regolith, re-shoot the 720p set against it, and assert on the screenshot that label-on-panel contrast is at least 4.5 : 1.

- **Severity:** major (artifact: major, photo: minor)

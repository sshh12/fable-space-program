## Problem
The independent review of planet-bake led to re-bakes that change three things your generator reads from
planet-bake's outputs:

1. **Elevation bounds.** Phobos and Enceladus now carry a global DEM (review finding 3) instead of the bare
   ellipsoid, so their baked `layers.height.elevation` (m above `manifest.radius`) is wider than the range of
   your staged terrain sections:
   - Phobos: -2,927.9 .. +2,938.8 (HRSC global DEM about its 11.1 km sphere; staged -1,987 .. 1,922)
   - Enceladus: -4,470.1 .. +4,726.5 (Bland et al. 2020 shape model; staged -3,955 .. 4,531)
   verify.py's check "staged terrain section's elevation range contains the baked one" fails for both.
2. **Mean colours** (`data/staged/render/albedo-means.json`, regenerated by verify.py): after the lead's decision
   every body's level-0 mean luminance is its configured albedo within 2 % (highlights through a soft knee;
   docs/handoff/planet-bake.md decision 20), so the luminances changed a lot (the bright moons by up to 3x)
   while the chromaticities moved little, except Triton's (its Voyager colour map is now white-balanced to
   B-V 0.70). Enceladus' configured albedo is now 0.77 (the most its map holds under the ceiling; geometric
   albedo 1.0): if your far disc keeps the geometric albedo, the near tiles hand over at 0.77.
3. **Credits** (`data/staged/credits.json`, for bodies-activation): Mercury's albedo credit
   `usgs-mercury-mdis-bdr` is replaced by `usgs-mercury-mdis-loi`; new `dlr-phobos-hrsc-dem` and
   `usgs-enceladus-bland-2020-shape`; the two OPAL lines now say the maps were modified (CC BY 4.0). The Phobos DEM's licence is the third class the lead accepted: a provider statement of no access or
   use constraints, credited.

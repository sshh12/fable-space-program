## Problem
The lead's decision on request bodies-ephemeris-4 (item 4) takes the far-field mean colour
(`render.meanColor`) of the bodies with no published colour index from the global colour maps planet-bake
bakes. Io and Ganymede have colour maps, and the generator now takes their area-weighted mean linear colour
from your cached sources (`tools/bodies/mapcolour.py` on `data/cache/planets/{io,ganymede}/dl/...`, read only):
Io (0.359, 0.248, 0.114), Ganymede (0.193, 0.179, 0.147). The other seven have no colour map: Europa,
Callisto and Iapetus are monochrome mosaics that your bake tints, and Mimas, Miranda, Ariel and Umbriel are
procedural. Their staged mean colour is neutral grey at the geometric albedo until the far impostor can match
what you bake.

## Evidence
`tools/bodies/sources/colours.json` (the seven rows carry that note); `tools/bake/planet/bodies/*.json`
`albedo.tint` / `albedo.procedural.color` for the seven.

## Requested change
When the level-0 albedo of these bodies is baked, report the mean LINEAR RGB of each body's level-0 albedo
(the six level-0 tiles decoded from sRGB, each texel weighted by its solid angle) for Europa, Callisto,
Iapetus, Mimas, Miranda, Ariel and Umbriel (and Io and Ganymede as a cross-check), e.g. as a small JSON file
under your paths or in your hand-off. The owner of `tools/bodies` (bodies-ephemeris now, solar-system-sim in
wave 4) then reads it into `tools/bodies/sources/colours.json` and regenerates; the generator keeps the
chromaticity and scales the luminance to the body's geometric albedo.

## Addendum (after the review of bodies-ephemeris)
1. Iapetus: its staged geometric albedo is the JPL Horizons value 0.6, its bright hemisphere only (NSSDC lists
   0.05 / 0.5 for the two hemispheres). Please include in the report its DISC-mean linear colour AND the
   disc-mean albedo of the baked level-0 map (solid-angle weighted), so the far impostor carries the
   two-tone body's mean rather than its bright side.
2. Large procedural craters: the generic models' crater population uses one depth-to-diameter ratio at every
   size (`freshDepthToDiameter` 0.196 x D, src/terrain/craters.ts), and your configs extend it to
   `maxDiameterRadii` 0.4 R; on Titania, Oberon, Ariel and Umbriel that is a 300-316 km basin 47-62 km deep,
   while basins that size are complex craters a few km deep. The staged terrain sections' `minElevation`
   follows what the bake produces (Titania -63.6 km, Oberon -61.4, Ariel -47.9, Umbriel -47.1), so it is right
   for your bake but the relief is not. If you move large craters to a complex-crater depth (or cap the depth),
   say so; the generator recomputes the bounds from your parameters, and from your manifests'
   `layers.height.elevation` as soon as they exist.

## Resolution
1. **Mean colours.** `data/staged/render/albedo-means.json` (tracked; regenerated by every
   `python -I tools/bake/planet/verify.py` run): for every baked solid body the mean LINEAR RGB and luminance of
   its level-0 albedo (the six tiles decoded from sRGB, texels weighted by solid angle, max|d_i|^3), the disc
   mean over the whole sphere, and `colourBasis`, which says plainly where the colour comes from.
   - Europa, Callisto, Iapetus: the maps are MONOCHROME; their tint is now derived from the published
     disc-integrated B-V (Harris 1961 via Pace 1971: Europa 0.87, Callisto 0.86, Iapetus whole disc 0.71) with
     the stated rule (relative reflectance B/V from Delta(B-V) against the Sun's 0.65, R/V by continuing the
     slope linearly in wavelength); `albedo.tintRef` in each config.
   - Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel (and Titania, Oberon): procedural; their colour is now from Karkoschka 2001
     (Icarus 151:51, HST): B-V and V-R against his solar 0.65 / 0.53. Mimas: no measured colour index exists
     (Pace 1971 has only bracketed values; Morrison & Cruikshank 1974: photographic magnitudes), so it stays
     neutral grey, said so in its config.
   - Values now (linear RGB; luminance): Europa (0.560, 0.477, 0.389; 0.488), Callisto (0.173, 0.149,
     0.123; 0.152), Iapetus DISC mean (0.320, 0.308, 0.293; 0.310: the two-tone body, not its bright side),
     Mimas (0.629 grey), Miranda (0.336, 0.338, 0.337), Ariel (0.418, 0.408, 0.404), Umbriel (0.222, 0.222,
     0.221), Titania (0.290, 0.282, 0.269), Oberon (0.248, 0.240, 0.233); cross-check Io (0.452, 0.313, 0.144)
     and Ganymede (0.306, 0.285, 0.235): the same chromaticity as your mapcolour.py values (R/G 1.44 and 1.07,
     B/G 0.46 and 0.83). Luminances are the bake's calibration (geometric albedo, capped so at most 0.5 % of a
     body clips in 8-bit), so take the chromaticity, as your generator does.
2. **Large procedural craters.** Above the simple-to-complex transition the baked crater depth follows a
   complex-crater law: D_t = 39.7 g^-0.4 km (g in cm/s^2; the icy-body fit of Aponte-Hernandez et al. 2021,
   PSJ 2:235, as stated in Bottke et al. 2024, eq. 3): Mimas 18.9, Miranda 17.5, Ariel and Umbriel 11.0,
   Titania 9.4, Oberon 9.5 km; fresh complex depth d = 0.366 D^0.559 km (Schenk 1989, JGR 94:3813, the Rhea fit
   as reproduced in Stern, Porter & Zangari 2015, arXiv:1412.1405; Cassini complex slopes 0.49-0.59 on Rhea,
   Iapetus and Mimas, White & Schenk 2011); a crater keeps its degradation share, the blend runs from D_t to
   2 D_t, the population is unchanged (`tools/bake/planet/procedural.ts`, `height.craters.complex` in the six
   configs). Re-baked with verify (666 checks, 0 failed) and the contact sheets, looked at: the 300 km basins
   are a few km deep. New bounds in the manifests' `layers.height.elevation` (m): Titania -7,313..1,206, Oberon
   -6,067..1,020, Ariel -5,813..2,766, Umbriel -5,716..1,109, Miranda -5,446..4,819, Mimas -9,626..9,858
   (Herschel 9.4 km deep). Deimos has no crater above its transition. The run-time model is unchanged (its
   craters stop at 4 postings, far below D_t). Please regenerate the terrain sections' bounds from the manifests.
